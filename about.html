About

I’m a Senior Product Designer focused on designing scalable SaaS products and complex systems—turning ambiguous problems into clear, usable solutions.

Over the past 5+ years, I’ve worked across HVAC, education, and platform tools, designing products used by professionals, students, and internal teams. My work often sits at the intersection of complex workflows, data, and operational constraints, where clarity, usability and systems thinking are critical.

How I Work

I’m comfortable working in ambiguous environments, identifying high-leverage problems, and driving projects from early discovery through to delivery and iteration.

My approach is grounded in understanding real user workflows and constraints. I use a mix of interviews, usability testing, and lightweight validation to ensure decisions are informed by actual user behavior—not assumptions.

I work closely with product managers and engineers to shape problem definitions, align on trade-offs, and deliver solutions that balance usability, speed, and technical feasibility. I care about building products that not only work well in isolation, but integrate cleanly into broader systems.
